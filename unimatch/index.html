Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and using UniMatch on campus.

What is UniMatch? expand_more

UniMatch is a student-only connection platform for verified Delhi college students to meet friends, study partners, and peers with shared passions in a secure and respectful environment.

Who can join UniMatch? expand_more

UniMatch is exclusively built for currently enrolled college students across Delhi-NCR universities (DU, IPU, DTU, IIT Delhi, NSUT, Jamia, and more).

How does student ID verification work? expand_more

When setting up your profile, you upload your official college ID card or authenticate with your college email address. Our moderation team reviews the ID to ensure 100% student authenticity before granting discovery access.

Is UniMatch free to use? expand_more

Yes! UniMatch is completely free for verified students. There are no paywalls for matching, seeing mutual admirers, or sharing Instagram handles.

Is UniMatch part of UniThrift? expand_more

Yes. UniMatch is UniThrift’s dedicated campus-connections and student community ecosystem. You can seamlessly switch between the Marketplace and UniMatch with a single click.
